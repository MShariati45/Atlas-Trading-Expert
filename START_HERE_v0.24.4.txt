ATLAS v0.24.4 — PAPER COST GATE ENABLED (SHADOW ONLY)

Purpose
-------
Enable continuous shadow/paper Supervisor decisions using the validated official
news backbone plus a conservative paper-only spread policy derived from the
2026-08-21 read-only broker calibration. MT5 order transmission remains hard locked.

Windows sequence
----------------
1. Open MT5 demo and keep it connected.
2. Run: run_official_four_currency_news_refresh.bat
3. Run: run_shadow_paper_supervisor.bat

Expected
--------
- broker_cost_policy_approved: true
- broker_cost_policy_mode: PAPER_ONLY
- execution_cost_validated: false
- execution_enabled: false
- A mature otherwise-valid candidate may receive an APPROVE paper decision only
  when the observed spread is positive and within the approved paper threshold.
- A zero/non-positive spread forces WAIT (NONPOSITIVE_SPREAD_UNVERIFIED).
- Slippage remains unmeasured and no paper approval can unlock MT5 execution.

Paper spread thresholds (P95 of the one-hour read-only calibration)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
EURUSD: 1 point
USDJPY: 3 points
USDCAD: 1 point
XAUUSD: 44 points

These are temporary paper thresholds, not permanent live-execution limits.
