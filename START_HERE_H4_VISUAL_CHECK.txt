ATLAS H4 VISUAL VALIDATION CHECK

1. Copy your existing historical_data folder into this Atlas folder.
2. Run validate_h4_eurusd_windows.bat first.
3. Then double-click generate_h4_validation_chart_windows.bat.
4. Your browser will open the EURUSD H4 validation chart.
5. Send a screenshot of that chart to ChatGPT.

This tool is read-only and cannot place trades.
