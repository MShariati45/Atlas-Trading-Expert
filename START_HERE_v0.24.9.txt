4. A candidate H4 HL/LH becomes strategic only after continuation creates a fresh HH/LL beyond the prior endpoint.
5. If the correction is still developing and no fresh extreme exists, Atlas preserves the LAST COMPLETED H4 impulse and reports CORRECTION/RANGE.
6. Repeated structural pivot zones and wick rejection remain active for the Gold teaching case.
7. Four H4 teaching benchmarks are audit-only: XAUUSD, EURUSD, USDJPY, USDCAD. Prices are never fed into the detector.
