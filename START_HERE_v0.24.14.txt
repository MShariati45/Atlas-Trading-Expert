ATLAS v0.24.14 - M15 CANDLESTICK REVERSAL CHALLENGER
=====================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Read-only one-year research on four symbols:
  EURUSD, USDJPY, USDCAD, XAUUSD

The challenger tests a deliberately small set of M15 reversal candlestick patterns:
  * Hammer / bullish pin bar
  * Shooting star / bearish pin bar
  * Bullish engulfing
  * Bearish engulfing
  * Morning star
  * Evening star

A signal is eligible only when:
  1) simple recent-impulse H4 direction agrees with the trade direction, and
  2) the pattern is near a broker-native D1/H4/H1 support/resistance reaction zone.

H1 DIRECTION IS NOT A GATE.
M15 TREND CLASSIFICATION IS NOT REQUIRED.

The runner compares:
  * EARLY entry at pattern close
  * CHOCH-CONFIRMED entry within 4 M15 bars
  * with and without an M15 counter-move filter
  * fixed targets at 2R, 3R, and 4R

It also reports an Atlas portfolio simulation with:
  * maximum 2 trades per Vancouver day
  * maximum 1 trade per symbol per day

SAFETY
------
This package is research-only.
It sends NO orders.
It does NOT modify the Shadow Paper Supervisor state.
It does NOT enable the candlestick agent for demo execution.

HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Keep MetaTrader 5 open and logged in to the demo/broker account whose history you want to test.
2. Extract this ZIP into a new folder.
3. Double-click:
       run_m15_candlestick_challenger.bat
4. Wait for all four symbols and all four timeframes to finish collecting/analyzing.
5. Upload the folder:
       runtime\candlestick_challenger
   or at minimum upload:
       REPORT.md
       candlestick_summary.csv
       candlestick_trade_events.csv
       candlestick_challenger_report.json

IMPORTANT RESEARCH RULES
------------------------
* Same-bar SL/TP ambiguity is counted conservatively as a LOSS.
* Stops use the entire candlestick pattern/recent M15 structural invalidation plus a buffer.
* Support/resistance and H4 direction are calculated using bars available at the signal cutoff only.
* Do not promote a pattern/configuration from a tiny sample just because its win rate looks high.
* The goal is to discover whether this challenger adds measurable value before connecting it to demo execution.
